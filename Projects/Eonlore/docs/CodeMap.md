# Eonlore code map

Eonlore's game code (`Game/src`) and its scene and art tools (`scripts/`): the game half of Hydro's `docs/CodeMap.md`. Engine, editor and runtime files are in the engine's code map; the rules for game code are in the engine's `docs/Scripting.md`.

## Game: `Game/src`

The game's own code (the Sandbox project's scripts), a static library linked by Oxygen and the Runtime. Its pre-build step runs the reflection generator. Its planned shape (shared `Data/`, `State/`, `Rules/`, `Views/`, `Scripts/Common/`; `Scripts/<Area>/` for what only one area or scene uses) and the rules for game scripts are in the wiki's [Scripting plan](https://github.com/joelnl74/Hydro/wiki/Scripting-Plan) (the files moved there Nov 1, 2026, SC8a: `State/`, `Rules/`, `Views/`, `Scripts/Common/`, `Scripts/Field/`, `Scripts/Battle/`, one folder per area, `Scripts/Examples/` and `Scripts/Tests/`; the scripts are still split further in SC8e to g).

## Game tools: `scripts/`

`scripts/game/fonts/make_bmfont.py <font.ttf> --size <px> --out <Assets path without extension>`: bakes a TTF / OTF into a BMFont (`.fnt` + one PNG page, pixel fonts without anti-aliasing at their native size, `--smooth` for others; ASCII, Latin-1 and typographic punctuation; kerning) and writes the page's `.hymeta` (Nearest for a pixel font, Linear with `--smooth`; Clamp) and the font's, with fresh handles, when missing (Pillow, fontTools). `Assets/Fonts`: `PixelifySans11` (11 px), and the game's smooth fonts `EBGaramond30`, `EBGaramond22`, `Cinzel34`, `Cinzel20` (`--smooth --page-width 512 --padding 2`, from the OFL fonts' Latin subsets; licences next to them).

`scripts/game/ui/make_ui_art.py`: draws `Assets/UI` (the runtime UI's frames, ribbons, cursor and markers, the quest marker `QuestMark` and the quest banner's `Band`; Pillow). `scripts/game/ui/build_game_ui.py [battle] [field]`: rebuilds the runtime UI of `Battle.hyscene`, `ShrineGlade.hyscene`, `FarmVillage.hyscene` and `FarmVillageInn.hyscene` (the field scenes' includes the `Quest Canvas`: tracker and banner) (removes its own and the replaced entities by tag, appends them again with IDs from their tags; close the scenes first; PyYAML). `scripts/game/ui/preview_ui.py <scene> --out file.png [--canvas name] [--show / --hide name] [--focus button] [--world "Canvas=x,y"] [--background image] [--size w h]`: draws a scene's runtime UI offline as the engine lays it out (rects, layout groups, scroll views, nine-slice, BMFont text, tints, focus visibility).

`scripts/game/quests/build_shrine_quest.py`: adds the Shrine Glade's quest to `ShrineGlade.hyscene`: `Quest Director` (`ShrineQuestScript`) under `Directors` with its `Cinematic Canvas` (letterbox) and `Quest Marker Canvas`, and three dormant slimes `Slime Grounds 1` to `3` under `Characters` / `Slimes Lower Path`; uses `build_game_ui.py`'s helpers and `rebuild` (removes its own entities by tag and appends them again, IDs from the tags; run `build_game_ui.py field` first, close the scene first; PyYAML). See [Shrine Glade quest](TestBed.md#shrine-glade-quest).

`scripts/game/quests/build_countryside_quest.py`: adds the countryside's quests to `JapaneseCountryside.hyscene`: `Countryside Quest` (`CountrysideQuestScript`) with the `Leyline 0` to `18` points and `Ley Marker Canvas`, `Ley Tree` (`Sprites/Trees/ancient_tree.png`, 10 m), `Npc Orin` (his stand-in field sheet), `Ley Wisp 1` to `3`, the way down (`Ley Rift`, `Ley Rift Glow`, the `Leyline Gate` trigger), `Arrive ShrineGlade`, and `Side Quests` (`QuestGiverScript`) with the givers' `Quest Marker <Giver>` canvases; heights from the scene builder's `ground_z`. `build_countryside.py` runs it (after `scripts/game/ui/build_game_ui.py field`); idempotent.

`scripts/game/japanese_city/build_countryside.py [--profile Day|Dusk|Night] [--start north|south] [--map-only] [--no-extras]` (Nov 2, 2026): the Japanese countryside on a terrain, the largest field scene (140 x 200 m): writes `Terrains/JapaneseCountryside.hyterrain` (four earth-banked terraces rising north with ramps where the road and paths cross, the mountain to the north with a stone-lined cut and a landing for the shrine stairs (built-in Stairs meshes), sunk paddies with seedling decor, the raised cedar forest, the pond) and the whole `JapaneseCountryside.hyscene` (the paddies' water and blockers, the woods with solid trunks, the fox shrine, the pond, the farm hub and bamboo grove, the ronin camp, the NPCs and side-quest givers, gather spots, slimes and wisps, exits and arrivals, camera zones, the backdrop), a preview map in `scripts/game/terrain/out/`, then runs the field UI and quest builders. `ground_z(x, y)` is the ground height without building the terrain. `scripts/game/japanese_city/make_countryside_art.py`: the sprites it needed that the project lacked (Pillow): bamboo clumps, the camp's tarp, the fox statue, a bamboo shoot, the leyline rift and its glow.

`scripts/game/interiors/build_inn.py`: writes the Farm Village inn's interiors, `FarmVillageInn.hyscene` (the common room, stairs up on the west wall) and `FarmVillageInnUpstairs.hyscene` (the guest rooms with the beds, reached through `Door Upstairs`): placeholder rooms of built-in shapes with the kit's textures, their `Assets/Materials/Interior` and `RenderProfiles/Interiors` when missing, then the field UI through `build_game_ui.py`; stable handles and IDs; close the scenes first; PyYAML. `scripts/check_texel_density.py <scene> [--assets DIR] [--target 32] [--tolerance 0.2] [--all] [--strict]`: lists the sprites, shape materials and model materials (`.glb` UVs) of a scene drawn outside the world density (32 texels per metre, [Sprite sizes](SpriteSizes.md#world-density-32-texels-per-metre-decided-2026-10-30)); PyYAML, numpy for models.

`scripts/snap_pixel_texture.py in.png out.png [--period N] [--scale F] [--colors N] [--no-tile]`: turns "fake pixel art" (art pixels drawn as N x N blocks) into a clean texture: snaps to the real grid, makes organic surfaces tile (edge fold), quantises to a palette, prints the material Tiling for 32 texels per metre (Pillow, numpy).
